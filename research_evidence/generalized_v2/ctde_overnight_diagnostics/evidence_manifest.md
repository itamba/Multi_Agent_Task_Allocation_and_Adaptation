## 4. Known anomalies and limitations

- **`traceback_in_log = True` on every one of the three `CHECK` lines in `sweep_log.txt`.**
  This is a naive `Select-String -Pattern 'Traceback|CRASH'` grep over the full console log
  (`run_sweep.ps1` line 46), and it is **not** a stop condition by itself — the driver only
  stops on a non-zero exit code, a manifest/HEAD/dirty mismatch, or a missing
  `run_summary.json`, none of which occurred (`exit=0` on all three `ARM END` lines).
  Inspection of the matched text (`official_console.log`, all three runs) shows the tracebacks
  are exactly the eight expected, accounted `FuelDamageError: no_fd_eligible_ego` /
  `EpisodeAttemptError("setup", ...)` exceptions per run, printed by ordinary Python exception
  handling — the same kind of expected setup attrition
  [`environments_cleanup.md` §1](../../../docs/workflows/environments_cleanup.md#1-execution-contexts)
  and [`experiments.md` §6](../../../docs/workflows/experiments.md#6-standing-constraints)
  say must not be converted into an environment or run failure. This matches each run's own
  `failures_by_error_type = {"FuelDamageError": 8}` exactly. Not treated as an anomaly
  requiring escalation; recorded here for transparency since the raw log flag reads alarming
  out of context.
- **`smallbatch`'s eval rounds at `updates_completed = 200` and `250`** are outliers versus
  every other `post_update` round in all three runs. `diagnostic_summary.json`'s
  `eval_trajectory` entries are 8-tuples
  `(stage, updates_completed, eval_reward_mean, eval_reward_mean_mild, eval_reward_mean_severe,
  macro_mean_over_base_cells, eval_deaths, meta_action_counts["SELF_PRESERVATION_ABORT"])`
  (field order fixed by `sweep/summarize_arm.py`). Every other `post_update` round in all
  three runs reads `(..., -0.220908, -0.00625, -0.650223, <near-zero>, 20, 0)`,
  but rounds 200 and 250 both read `(..., -0.259527, -0.385162, -0.38717, <near-zero>, 0, 40)`:
  the mild and severe reward means move much closer together (`-0.385` / `-0.387` instead of
  `-0.006` / `-0.650`), `eval_deaths` drops from the otherwise-constant `20` to `0`, and the
  round's total `SELF_PRESERVATION_ABORT` count rises from `0` to `40` (double the `20` the
  final round's own `meta_action_counts` reports). The primary endpoint itself
  (`macro_mean_over_base_cells`) stays of the same near-zero magnitude as every other round
  (`~1.8e-6`, versus e.g. `~1.6e-6` at round 150), so this excursion is in the auxiliary
  reward/death/action-mix metrics, not in the primary endpoint. It occurs **only** in
  `smallbatch` and **only** at these two consecutive rounds, reverting by round 300. This
  preservation task did **not** investigate the cause — it is restated here exactly as
  `diagnostic_summary.json` reports it, as a fact for GPT to inspect, not as a diagnosed
  defect or a claimed anomaly resolution. The run's own `accounting_check.json` still reports
  `verdict: PASS` and `n_eval_rounds = 16`, so whatever produced these two rounds' numbers did
  not break the run's overall accounting, and both rounds' underlying per-episode records are
  present in `smallbatch/eval_records.jsonl` (committed) for direct inspection.
- **`episode_outcomes.jsonl` was not committed for any of the three runs** (§1, §5) — a
  scoped, stated choice under the task's own branching instruction, not an omission by
  oversight.
- **`scenarios/` was not individually hashed** (3968 files × 3 runs ≈ 11,900 files); only an
  aggregate file count and byte total is recorded per run in `artifact_sha256.txt`.
- **`summary_ctde_r1_pr62.json` was not independently re-verified against PR #62's committed
  evidence by this task** beyond confirming its content is internally consistent with the
  numbers PR #62's own body and prior review already state (R1's `updates_completed = 375`,
  `n_eval_rounds = 16`, the near-null primary endpoint). It was generated in a prior session
  from a local scratchpad copy of PR #62's data, not regenerated by this task.
